# Campaign artefacts (proof)

Drop **real** campaign screenshots here and they'll appear in the "Artefacts
from the field" marquee automatically (refresh / redeploy). No stock photos,
these should look like artefacts from real campaigns: TikTok/Instagram
analytics, reach numbers, creator content, community screenshots, campaign
timelines.

**The filename becomes the caption.** Use hyphens as separators:

- `tiktok-4.2m-views-72h.png` → `TIKTOK · 4.2M · VIEWS · 72H`
- `instagram-reach-1.1m.png` → `INSTAGRAM · REACH · 1.1M`
- `community-4300-posts.png` → `COMMUNITY · 4300 · POSTS`

**Formats:** `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.webp`, `.avif`, `.gif`

They're shown greyscale by default and reveal to full colour on hover, so they
sit inside the monochrome aesthetic rather than looking like marketing blocks.
With no images here, the section simply doesn't render.
